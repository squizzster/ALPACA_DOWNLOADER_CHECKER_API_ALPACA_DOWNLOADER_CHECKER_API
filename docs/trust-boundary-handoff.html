Trust-boundary implementation handoff

Read this before continuing the boundary audit or changing handoffs. This is the repository copy of the implementation review, including the exact remaining scope. The work changes trust boundaries, handoff retention, producer ingress validation, progress carry-through and acknowledgement bookkeeping. Lineage rules and correlation policy are unchanged; reuse consumes completed pricing and metadata handoffs.

The finding register distinguishes verified repairs from remaining requirements. F09 remains open, F15 remains partial, F13 retains its clarified supported scope, and F07 retains fresh negative observations on each invocation. Synthetic verification does not establish production throughput.

Authorities and continuation

AGENTS.md owns storage trust and permissions. Architecture identifies the producer for each boundary. Technical contracts owns the artifact, lock and recovery guarantees. Development owns verification and rule-change requirements. CLI reports explains heartbeats and debug traces.

Before changing a handoff, identify its producer, selecting artifact, scope, lifetime and actual invalidation condition. Preserve an accepted value until one of those conditions changes. Retain the owning regressions that forbid repeated upstream reads and exercise newer generations, incomplete publication and expired authority. In particular, a passing suite does not close the remaining historical attribution or every coverage gap.

The separate standalone Bloodhound converter consumes completed CSV exports or legacy histories; it does not participate in pricing, lineage or serving publication. Its current CSV regressions assert selected-only consumption, one read per selected boundary/payload and no upstream checksum recalculation. CSV schema 2 carries the opened catalogue's completed observed-label ranges intact, including for filtered exports. The converter projects their inclusive endpoints once into row labels shared across all eight views; it does not replay ownership evidence, audit descriptive ticker membership or reopen the source catalogue. Missing schema-2 handoffs fail before payload reads; actual ambiguity remains explicit and may be resolved by supplied dated metadata. Schema-1 compatibility scopes older ownership intervals to the full input range and actual sessions before interpreting them. The regressions cover schema/generation mismatch, missing and empty handoffs, overlapping labels, exact endpoints, one-session conflicts and forbidden repeated selection, source authentication or boundary reads. The CSV producer regressions also cover an end-to-end export/conversion and retain publication interruption and source-read guards. Its known publication and validation limits are separate from the engine findings below. Importing the app does not close those limits or extend the engine's publication guarantees to it.

Raw traces, runtime results and experiments remain outside Git. Their previously accepted structural integrity is retained; the code repair does not repeat that acceptance. The untraced older four-hour run still lacks exact attribution (F09), and test coverage remains finite (F15). These results do not authorize suppressing genuine changed-price, changed-ownership or changed-mapping work.
